## Privacy Policy

This is a personal academic website. It is a static site hosted on
[GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/) and is intended purely to share
research, publications, and professional information.

### What this site collects

**Nothing directly.** This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, serves no
advertising, and has no comment system or contact forms. I do not collect,
store, or share any personal information about visitors.

### Hosting logs

Because the site is served by GitHub Pages, GitHub may automatically log basic
technical request information (such as IP address, browser type, and time of
access) as part of operating its infrastructure. This data is handled by GitHub
under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
and is not accessible to or controlled by me.

### External links

Pages here link to third-party services (for example Google Scholar, ORCID,
GitHub, publisher websites, and hosted PDFs/slides). Those sites have their own
privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them.
